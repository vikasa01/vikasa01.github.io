Privacy policy

Home Control is a private app used only by its owner and his household. This policy covers the Google account access it requests (Google Nest Device Access).

What it accesses

With the account owner's consent, Home Control reads the status of the owner's own Nest devices (for example the doorbell) and shows its camera stream to the owner. It does not access email, contacts, files or any other Google data.

How data is used and stored

Device data is used only to display and control the owner's devices. Sign-in tokens are stored on the owner's own computer and are never shared. Home Control does not sell, share, transfer or publish any data, and does not use it for advertising or analytics.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
